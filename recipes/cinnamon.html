Grandma's Cinnamon Swirl Loaf

This warmly spiced cinnamon loaf fills the kitchen with the smell of butter, brown sugar and cinnamon as it bakes. It has a soft, tender crumb and a sweet cinnamon swirl running through every slice, making it perfect with a cup of tea or coffee.

Ingredients

  • 2 1/2 cups (375 g) plain flour
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 115 g unsalted butter, softened
  • 3/4 cup (165 g) caster sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 cup (250 ml) buttermilk
  • 1/2 cup (110 g) brown sugar
  • 2 tablespoons ground cinnamon
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180°C (160°C fan). Grease and line a 22 cm x 12 cm loaf tin with baking paper, leaving some paper over the sides to help lift the loaf out.
  2. Whisk the flour, baking powder, baking soda and salt together in a medium bowl.
  3. Beat the softened butter and caster sugar in a large bowl until pale and creamy. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then mix in the vanilla.
  4. Add the flour mixture in three additions, alternating with the buttermilk. Mix gently after each addition until just combined. Do not overmix.
  5. In a small bowl, stir together the brown sugar and cinnamon.
  6. Spoon about half of the batter into the prepared loaf tin and smooth the surface. Sprinkle evenly with half of the cinnamon sugar, then drizzle with half of the melted butter.
  7. Add the remaining batter and spread gently to cover the cinnamon layer. Sprinkle with the remaining cinnamon sugar and drizzle with the remaining melted butter.
  8. Run a butter knife through the batter in a few gentle zigzags to create a swirled pattern. Do not over-swirl or the layers will disappear.
  9. Bake for 45 to 55 minutes, until the loaf is golden and a skewer inserted into the centre comes out clean or with a few moist crumbs attached.
  10. Leave in the tin for 10 minutes, then lift out using the baking paper and transfer to a wire rack. Cool for at least 20 minutes before slicing.

Serve warm or at room temperature, with butter if you like. Makes 1 loaf, about 10 to 12 slices.
