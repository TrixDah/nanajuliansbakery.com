Nana's Buttermilk Scones

Every Sunday morning Nana had a tray of these cooling on the windowsill before anyone else was awake. They are tall, soft in the middle and golden on top, and they never last past lunch.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups (450 g) self-raising flour, plus extra for dusting
  • 1 tablespoon caster sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 60 g cold butter, cubed
  • 1 cup (250 ml) cold buttermilk
  • 1/2 cup (125 ml) cold milk, plus extra for brushing

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan) and put a baking tray inside to warm.
  2. Sift the flour, sugar and salt into a large bowl. Rub in the butter with your fingertips until it looks like coarse crumbs.
  3. Make a well in the middle. Pour in the buttermilk and most of the milk, then cut it through with a butter knife until just combined. Add the remaining milk only if the dough is dry.
  4. Tip onto a floured bench and pat gently into a rectangle about 3 cm thick. Do not knead. Overworking makes tough scones.
  5. Cut into rounds with a floured cutter, pressing straight down without twisting.
  6. Place on the hot tray with the sides just touching, and brush the tops with milk.
  7. Bake for 12 to 15 minutes until risen and golden. Wrap in a clean tea towel to keep the crust soft.

Serve warm with jam and thick cream. Makes about 12.
