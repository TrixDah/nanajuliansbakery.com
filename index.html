📜 Nana's Field Manual

1. March & explore

Only your homestead is visible when you spawn. Your whole brigade travels as one stack; each unknown plot is revealed as the soldiers pass through it. Moving onto unclaimed land claims it without paying bread. Rival baker factions scout, expand, and can attack your holdings.

2. Three separate inventories

The brigade carries bread, wheat and flour in separate inventories, each with its own capacity. Upgrade all three capacities at home. Fields produce wheat only while their owner keeps troops on the plot. Soldiers harvest wheat, carry it to a mill, and unload it. Each wheat becomes one flour. A timed task is cancelled if the brigade leaves before it finishes.

3. Work, bake & store

Milling and baking only progress while the brigade stays at that site and work is enabled. Leaving pauses the process; return and press Start/Resume to continue. Each flour makes one bread, but the bakery stops exactly when its bread storage is full and leaves any extra flour untouched. Only bread can go into the home treasury.

4. Upgrade & protect

Buildings, the brigade, carrying capacities and treasury can be upgraded through ten levels. Troop, carrying-capacity and treasury upgrades are home-only; building upgrades are performed at the site. Two-thirds of treasury bread and one-quarter of carried bread are marked protected. Only bread can be stored in the treasury.

Four autonomous rival factions can scout, capture plots, harvest, mill, bake, upgrade, and raid. The game runs entirely in this browser and saves locally.

🏆 The Great Bread Hoard

The richest baker holds the title Nana Julian's Bakery. Your score counts treasury bread, bread carried by the brigade, and finished bread waiting at your bakeries. Only bread counts toward your hoard; wheat and flour do not.
